# Real proteomics and metabolomics metadata

The final campaign qualified two additional public metadata routes because the previous Phase 2 modality receipt contained only constructed fixtures. PRIDE contributes proteomics projects; Metabolomics Workbench contributes study summaries. They are available through `litdatamatcher dataset-search --source pride` and `--source metabolomicsworkbench`. Queries and response sizes remain bounded; neither route retrieves measurements or raw files.

Official contracts: [PRIDE Archive API](https://www.ebi.ac.uk/pride/ws/archive/v2/docs/api-guide.html) and [Metabolomics Workbench REST specification](https://www.metabolomicsworkbench.org/tools/MWRestAPIv1.2.pdf). The actual September 13 responses, not just the documentation, establish the parsed schema. Exact source objects, request URLs, UTC retrieval times and hashes are retained outside Git.

The predeclared qualification queried PRIDE for `Alzheimer`, Workbench study titles for `Diabetes`, and the preserved `ST000001` accession. It obtained 25 PRIDE and 40 Workbench unique study identifiers. Explicit human, mouse and additional organism metadata allow real wrong-organism and wrong-modality challenges. Different project IDs do not establish independent cohorts. File names, summary sample counts and repository membership do not establish aligned features, usable controls, donor counts, feature units, or analysis readiness. Those capabilities stay unknown. Source-specific licenses remain explicit; no redistribution permission is inferred from public access.

The larger real corpus also exposed two provenance/lifecycle defects. Paginated rows must cite their own response page, rather than the final page. Europe PMC's `commentCorrection` container includes ordinary comments: only its explicit notice type can establish a correction/retraction, and unknown notice types require source review. Both have focused regressions. No frozen alpha artifact or sealed holdout was rerun.
